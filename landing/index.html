How it works

Just register a URL — 5 steps to your AI-search readiness.

1Register your siteSite name, public URL, and brand name.
2Technical GEO auditDeterministic checks of robots.txt, llms.txt, JSON-LD, meta, content structure, and freshness signals.
3Japanese AEO scoringAnswer-first writing, definitions, Q&A, evidence, readability, and search intent — scored independently for Japanese.
4LLM answer simulationAn LLM evaluates answerability and missing information grounded only in your page content.
5Fix and re-auditA 100-point score with evidence-backed recommendations, saved as history to track progress.

Built on two open-source projects
